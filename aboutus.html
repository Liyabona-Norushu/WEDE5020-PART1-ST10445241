Sip & Sack Co. was founded in 2024 with a straightforward concept: commonplace objects like canvas tote bags, stainless-steel tumblers, and enamel badges should be as distinctive as the individuals who use them. Our mission is to provide high-quality, personalized products, such as sturdy tumblers and tote bags, that enable individuals and regional companies to showcase their individual styles. Vision Statement: To become the leading online retailer of personalized lifestyle products in South Africa, with plans to grow into eco-friendly printed goods and large corporate purchases. Our Story

Founded in 2024, Sip & Sack Co. started as a small, hands-on shop specializing in custom stainless-steel tumblers, durable canvas tote bags, and vibrant enamel badges. In the beginning, every order was managed personally through social media messages, emails, and word-of-mouth recommendations.

As our products gained momentum beyond our local community, taking orders and tracking payments manually became overwhelming. To deliver a faster, reliable, and more effortless experience for our customers across South Africa, we launched our official online store—combining automated ordering, custom design previews, and secure checkout in one place.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, personalized products that allow students, young professionals, event planners, and local businesses to showcase their distinct personality and brand image.

Our Vision

To become the premier online retailer of personalized lifestyle products in South Africa, while expanding into eco-friendly printed goods and corporate wholesale merchandise.
